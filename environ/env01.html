Environment 01

A misty forest is quiet and mysterious, with tall trees disappearing into a thick layer of fog. Soft light filters through the branches, creating a dim and peaceful atmosphere. The ground is covered with moss, fallen leaves, and damp roots, while distant sounds of birds and rustling leaves echo through the trees. The fog makes the forest feel isolated, almost like there is something hidden just beyond what you can see.
